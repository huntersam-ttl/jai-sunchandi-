# Jai Supa Deurali Jewellers

Digital infrastructure for a family jewellery business in Kapilvastu, Nepal.

This project started from a real operational problem: too much of the shop workflow depended on notebooks, memory, and manual calculations. The goal is to give a traditional jewellery business a modern system without making day-to-day work harder for the people actually using it.

## What it does

The platform brings together the public storefront and the internal business workflow.

### Public experience

- live gold and silver rates
- jewellery catalogue and product detail pages
- WhatsApp enquiries
- QR-linked products and certificates
- custom order requests
- repair requests
- order-status lookup
- SEO-friendly product pages

### Business operations

- daily metal-rate management
- product and catalogue management
- customer khata / account tracking
- custom orders
- repairs
- old-gold exchange
- frozen-price order handling
- split and multi-payment records
- invoice archive
- certificate verification
- lead management
- reporting
- global search

## Jewellery pricing

The system is designed around the way jewellery is actually priced locally rather than treating every product like a normal e-commerce item.

A simplified pricing model is:

```text
metal value = weight × today's rate × purity

total =
  metal value
  + jarti / wastage
  + jyala / labour
  + other applicable charges
```

Rates and purity calculations are handled independently so pricing logic can work across different karats and changing daily metal prices.

## Why I built it

This is not a demo project built around an imaginary business.

It is an attempt to modernise the systems used by my family's jewellery shop while preserving the workflows that already make sense to the people running it.

The broader question behind the project is:

> How can small traditional businesses adopt better software without forcing them into software designed for completely different markets?

## Direction

The long-term aim is to keep improving the system around real shop usage, including:

- simpler day-to-day administration
- better customer records
- stronger trust through digital invoices and verification
- easier catalogue sharing
- clearer reporting
- more reliable operational data
- a stronger digital presence for the business

## Status

Active development.

The project has gone through repeated integration, security, pricing, deployment, and SEO work as the system has grown from a simple shop website into a broader operating platform.
